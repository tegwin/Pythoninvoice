# Creating Your First Invoice

This guide walks you through creating a professional invoice step by step.

## Prerequisites

Before creating an invoice, you should have:

- [x] Set up your company information
- [x] Added at least one customer
- [x] Added your products/services (optional but recommended)

## Creating an Invoice

### Step 1: Navigate to New Invoice

Click **Invoices** in the navigation menu, then click **➕ New Invoice**.

### Step 2: Select Customer

Choose a customer from the dropdown. Their details will be automatically filled in.

!!! tip "Quick Customer Add"
    If the customer doesn't exist yet, you can add them directly from the invoice form.

### Step 3: Set Invoice Details

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Invoice Number** | Auto-generated (e.g., INV-01001) |
| **Issue Date** | Today's date by default |
| **Due Date** | Typically 30 days from issue |
| **Currency** | Customer's preferred currency |
| **Tax Rate** | Default or customer-specific rate |

### Step 4: Add Line Items

Click **Add Item** to add products or services:

1. **Description**: What you're billing for
2. **Quantity**: Number of units
3. **Unit Price**: Price per unit
4. **Tax Rate**: Tax percentage for this item

The line total is calculated automatically.

#### Using Saved Products

If you've added products to your catalog:

1. Click the product dropdown
2. Select a product
3. The description and price are auto-filled
4. Adjust quantity as needed

### Step 5: Review Totals

The invoice summary shows:

- **Subtotal**: Sum of all line items (before tax)
- **Tax**: Total tax amount
- **Total**: Final amount due

### Step 6: Add Notes (Optional)

Use the notes field for:

- Payment instructions
- Terms and conditions
- Project references
- Thank you messages

### Step 7: Save the Invoice

Click **Create Invoice** to save. You can choose:

- **Save as Draft**: Edit later before sending
- **Create & Send**: Immediately email to customer

## Invoice Statuses

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 📝 **Draft** | Not yet sent, can be edited |
| 📤 **Sent** | Sent to customer, awaiting payment |
| 💰 **Partial** | Partially paid |
| ✅ **Paid** | Fully paid |
| ⚠️ **Overdue** | Past due date, unpaid |
| ❌ **Cancelled** | Voided invoice |

## After Creating

Once your invoice is created, you can:

- **📥 Download PDF**: Get a PDF copy
- **🖨️ Print**: Print the invoice
- **📧 Send Email**: Email to customer
- **✏️ Edit**: Modify the invoice (if draft)
- **💳 Record Payment**: Add a payment

## Tips for Professional Invoices

1. **Be Specific**: Clear descriptions help avoid confusion
2. **Itemize**: Break down services/products individually
3. **Set Clear Terms**: Include payment due date and methods
4. **Add Your Logo**: Upload in Settings → Branding
5. **Use Notes**: Thank customers or include important info

## Common Questions

??? question "Can I change the invoice number format?"
    Yes! Go to Settings → Company and modify the Invoice Prefix field.

??? question "How do I add a discount?"
    Add a line item with a negative amount, or adjust the unit price.

??? question "Can I duplicate an invoice?"
    Yes, from the invoice detail page, click "Duplicate" to create a copy.
