# Quick Start Guide

Get up and running with Invoice Manager in under 5 minutes!

## Step 1: Log In

After installation, navigate to `http://localhost:5000` and log in with your admin credentials.

![Login Screen](../assets/login.png)

## Step 2: Set Up Your Company

Go to **Settings** and fill in your company information:

1. **Company Name**: Your business name
2. **Address**: Your business address
3. **Email**: Business email for correspondence
4. **Phone**: Contact number
5. **Logo**: Upload your company logo

!!! tip "Branding"
    A complete company profile makes your invoices look more professional.

## Step 3: Add Your First Customer

Navigate to **Customers → Add Customer**:

1. Enter the customer's name
2. Add their email address
3. Fill in their address (optional)
4. Set any custom tax rates or currency
5. Click **Save Customer**

## Step 4: Add Products/Services

Go to **Products → Add Product**:

1. Enter the product/service name
2. Set the default price
3. Add a description
4. Choose if it's taxable
5. Click **Save Product**

## Step 5: Create Your First Invoice

Navigate to **Invoices → New Invoice**:

1. Select a customer from the dropdown
2. Set the invoice date and due date
3. Add line items (products/services)
4. Review the totals
5. Click **Create Invoice**

## Step 6: Send the Invoice

From the invoice detail page:

1. Click **📧 Send Email**
2. Review the email preview
3. Click **Send**

Your customer will receive a professional invoice email with a PDF attachment!
